Who I am

I enjoy building digital experiences with personality. Whether that means designing a polished landing page, crafting a modded project, or experimenting with fresh creative ideas, I care about clarity, mood, quality, and user experience in the final result, not money.

My interests sit at the intersection of design and software building. I like projects that blend visual polish with real utility and a sense of story.
